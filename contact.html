Frequently Asked Questions

How do I list my property on RentGoX?

Sign in to the RentGoX app, go to "Add Property," and fill in the required details, photos, and pricing. Your listing will go live after basic verification.

How do I request a refund for a Premium Service?

Email us at supportrentgox@gmail.com or use the form above with your transaction reference ID. Please review our Refund & Cancellation Policy for eligibility details.

Which payment methods are supported?

We accept UPI, debit/credit cards, and net banking through our secure PhonePe Payment Gateway integration.

Is my payment information safe?

Yes. RentGoX does not store your card, UPI PIN, or net-banking credentials. All payments are processed directly by our PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway partner. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my account or data?

Contact us via email or the form above with your registered phone number, and we will process your deletion request in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

How long does support take to respond?

We aim to respond to all queries within 2 business days during our support hours listed above.
