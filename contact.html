Frequently Asked Questions

About RentGoX

What is RentGoX?

RentGoX is a rental marketplace app for Android, launched from Agartala, Tripura. Owners list rooms, apartments, houses, PGs, hostels, shops and offices, and seekers browse, shortlist and contact owners directly from their phone.

Is RentGoX live now?

Yes. The Room / Flat category is live today, covering rooms, apartments, houses, PGs, hostels, shops and offices. More categories, such as cars, bikes, electronics and marriage halls, are being rolled out step by step.

Which places does RentGoX cover?

We started in Agartala, Tripura. In the app you can pick any Indian state or union territory to browse. What you see depends on the listings owners have added there, and it grows as more owners join.

How do I download the app?

RentGoX is available for Android on Google Play. You can also browse listings on this website without installing anything.

Is RentGoX free to use?

Yes for seekers: searching for rooms and contacting owners is completely free, and there is no Premium plan for seekers. Owners can list up to 2 properties for free, with 2 photos each. Optional Premium plans and Boosts for owners are paid.

Room seekers: searching

How do I search for a room?

Type a title or location in the search bar at the top of the Home screen. You can also filter by tapping type tabs like Room, Apartment, House, or PG.

How do I use filters?

Tap the tune icon on the right side of the search bar, choose a price range and tap Apply Filters.

How do I search by location?

Type the area name in the search bar (for example Agartala or Collegetilla) and all matching properties will show.

How do I see a property on the map?

Open the property and tap its location to see it on the map.

Why isn't anything showing up?

Check your internet connection. There may simply be no properties matching your current filter or type right now. Try tapping Refresh.

Do new properties show up automatically?

Yes. Once our team approves a property, it appears when you refresh the Seeker Home screen.

Room seekers: saving, comparing and sharing

How do I save a property?

Tap the heart on a property. All your saved properties are in the Favourite tab.

How do I remove a saved property?

Tap the heart on that property again to unsave it.

Will I be told when a saved property changes?

Yes. When you tap the heart on a property, you get a notification if its rent changes or the room becomes available again.

Can I compare properties?

Yes. You can compare up to 3 properties side by side.

How do I share a property?

Open the property, tap the share icon at the top right and choose Share with photo. The first photo goes together with the property details to WhatsApp or any app you pick.

Room seekers: contacting owners and visits

How do I call the owner?

Tap the Call Owner button on the Property Details screen. It opens your phone's dialer directly.

How do I contact the owner on WhatsApp?

Tap the WhatsApp button on the Property Details screen. It opens a chat with the owner along with a starter message.

Can I chat with the owner inside the app?

Yes. Use Contact Owner on a property to chat in the app. Seekers can send as many messages as they like. Chat messages are deleted automatically 10 days after they are sent.

The owner isn't replying.

RentGoX only gives you a way to contact the owner. Whether they reply is up to them. Try again later or check other listings.

The phone number shown seems wrong.

If you believe the number is wrong or fake, tell us through Contact Support or use the Report icon on the property, and we will check the listing.

How do I schedule a visit?

Open a property and tap the calendar button next to Contact Owner. Pick a date and time (at least 30 minutes from now and within the next 30 days), add an optional note, and tap Send. The owner gets a notification and will confirm or decline.

How will I know if the owner confirmed?

You get a notification when the owner confirms or declines. You can also tap the calendar button on that property to see the status: waiting, confirmed, or declined.

How do I cancel a visit request?

Open the property, tap the calendar button, and choose Cancel request. The owner will be told.

I cannot send a visit request.

Check these: you must be logged in; the time must be at least 30 minutes from now and within 30 days; you can have only one active request per property and no more than 5 pending requests at a time. You also cannot request a visit to your own listing.

Room seekers: saved searches and alerts

What are saved searches?

You can save your filters (type, location, price range, family type) and get a notification when a matching new property is approved.

How do I save a search?

First apply the filters you want on the Home screen. Then open the menu where Help & Support is, tap Saved searches & alerts, and choose Save current filters. You can save up to 10 searches.

How do I stop alerts for a saved search?

Open Saved searches & alerts and tap the delete icon next to that search.

Room seekers: account and notifications

I'm not getting notifications.

Check that notification permission is enabled for RentGoX in your phone settings, and make sure you have an internet connection.

I can't see any announcements.

There may not be an active announcement right now. When there is one, it appears automatically as a popup when you open the app.

How do I delete a notification?

Tap the bell icon to open the notification list. Tapping a notification there marks it read and removes it from the list.

How do I edit my profile?

Tap the pencil icon next to your name in the Profile section at the top of the Home screen. You can update your name and city there.

How do I change my phone number?

To change your seeker account's phone number, log out and log in again with the new number.

How do I log out?

You will find Logout at the bottom of the three-dot menu. Tap it to sign out.

How do I turn on Dark Mode?

There is a Dark Mode switch in the three-dot menu that you can turn on or off.

Room seekers: safety and reports

How do I report a fake property or wrong information?

Open the property and tap the flag icon at the top (Report this listing). Choose a reason and send it. Our team reviews every report and can warn the owner or remove the listing.

What does the Reviewed label mean?

It appears on properties that our team has reviewed and approved before they went live. It does not mean the owner's identity or ownership of the property has been verified, so please check a property in person before you pay anything.

What does “Replies within 1 hr” mean?

It shows how fast the owner usually answers chat messages. It appears only after the owner has replied at least 3 times and is based on recent replies.

Why did a property disappear from the list?

The owner may have marked it Booked or Rented. A listing is also hidden automatically if the owner does not confirm that the room is still available when we ask.

How do I open a property from a QR code?

Tap the QR scanner icon at the top of the Home screen and point the camera at the property's QR code.

Does RentGoX collect rent or deposit?

No. Rent and deposit are agreed and paid directly between you and the owner, and RentGoX is not a party to that arrangement. Always visit the property and meet the owner before paying any advance.

Property owners: uploading a property

How do I upload a property?

Open the app, choose Property Owner Login and sign in with Google. Tap the + button on your Dashboard, fill in the title, price, location, photos and other details, then tap Submit Property. Before you submit, you confirm that you are the owner or are authorised by the owner. Once our team approves it, it becomes visible to seekers.

Photos aren't uploading.

Check your internet connection first. The app automatically compresses large photos, but if it still fails, try again on Wi-Fi or a stronger mobile data connection.

I can't add the location.

Tap the GPS button and allow location permission when prompted. Make sure your phone's Location/GPS setting is turned on.

The form isn't submitting.

Check that all required fields (title, price, location, at least 1 photo, GPS) are filled in. Any missing field is highlighted in red.

How many photos can I upload?

On the Free plan you can upload 2 photos. Premium plans allow up to 10 photos depending on the plan.

How do I edit my property?

Go to the My Listings tab, tap Edit on that property, update the details and submit again.

Property owners: listing status

Why does my property show “Pending”?

Every new property must be reviewed and approved by our team before it goes live.

Why was my property rejected?

When a property is rejected, the reason is shown on the property card. You can fix the issue and resubmit.

How long does approval take?

Our team usually reviews within a few hours, though it may take a bit longer during busy periods.

How do I change the room status (Available / Booked)?

On the property card under My Listings, you will find Available, Booked and Not Available buttons to change the status.

How do I delete a property?

Tap Delete on that property card under My Listings, then confirm.

Do deleted listings count towards my limit?

Yes. If you delete a listing, its slot is not given back. For example, on the Free plan (2 properties), if you upload 2 and delete both, you cannot upload 2 more for free. This stays the same if you change your mobile number.

Property owners: Premium, Boost and limits

What benefits do Premium plans offer?

Premium plans offer more photo uploads, more property listings, video upload on some plans, and unlimited chat messages. Plans run for 6 months or 1 year and do not renew automatically.

How do I buy Premium?

Tap the Premium card on your Dashboard, choose a plan and tap Upgrade Now. You pay in the app with UPI, and your plan activates automatically as soon as the payment succeeds. The price is shown before you pay.

When does my Premium plan expire?

The Premium screen shows how many days or hours are left on your current plan.

What happens when my Premium plan expires?

Your account goes back to the Free plan and its limits apply. Properties above the Free limit are hidden, not deleted, and they appear again if you renew.

Which plan gives unlimited property listings?

The top-tier Premium plan includes unlimited property listings and video upload.

Is there a limit on chat messages?

Owners on the Free plan can send up to 20 chat messages per month, and the count restarts every month. Receiving messages is always unlimited. Premium owners can send unlimited messages.

What is a Boost?

A Boost is an optional paid service for owners. It shows your approved property in the Featured section at the top of the app for 1, 3, 7 or 15 days, so more seekers notice it. It does not guarantee enquiries.

How do I boost my property?

On your owner home screen, tap Boost on an approved property, choose the number of days and pay with UPI. The price of each option is shown before you pay. If a Boost is already running, the new one starts when it ends.

Can I get a refund for a Boost?

A Boost cannot be refunded once it is active. If you are charged twice, or the money is deducted but the Boost does not start, we refund it, normally within 5 to 7 business days. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

Property owners: visit requests, availability and replies

Where do I see visit requests?

Tap the calendar icon at the top of your Owner home screen. You will see requests from seekers with their name, phone number, date, time and note.

How do I confirm or decline a visit?

Tap Confirm or Decline on the request. The seeker gets a notification right away. You can also call the seeker from the same screen.

I am not getting visit request notifications.

Make sure notifications are allowed for RentGoX and that you are logged in as an owner on this phone. You can still see every request by opening the calendar icon.

What does “Is this room still available?” mean?

If you do not confirm a listing for 7 days, we ask whether the room is still available. Tap Yes, available to keep it visible, or No, taken if it is rented.

Why was my listing hidden from seekers?

If you do not answer within 3 days, the listing is hidden so seekers do not call about a room that may be taken. Open My Properties and tap Available to show it again.

Will seekers know if I change the rent?

Yes. After your edit is approved, seekers who saved your property with the heart get a notification about the new rent.

What is the “Replies within 1 hr” badge?

When you answer seeker chats quickly, a green badge appears on your listings. It shows after you have replied at least 3 times and is based on your recent replies.

Property owners: notifications and account

Why am I not receiving notifications?

Check that notification permission is enabled for RentGoX in your phone settings, and make sure you have an internet connection.

Will I get notified when my property is approved?

Yes. You are notified through the bell icon and your phone's notification tray when a property is approved or rejected.

Where do I see new announcements?

New announcements pop up automatically when you open the app. If you dismiss one, it shows again after 24 hours while the offer is still active.

How do I delete a notification?

Swipe any notification in the list to see a delete confirmation, then tap Yes to remove it.

How do I change my name?

Go to the three-dot menu on your Dashboard, tap Edit Name, enter your new name and save.

How do I change my mobile number?

Go to Settings and tap Request Mobile Number Change, enter the new number and submit. You can change your number once. Our team verifies the request, and then the new number is updated on your account and on your listings. Your listing limit does not reset: your current listings and the listings you deleted earlier still count, under your old and new numbers together.

Google Sign-In isn't working.

Make sure at least one Google account is added on your phone and that you have an internet connection.

Property owners: support

How do I ask the team for help?

On your Dashboard, tap the Support banner to request a call. Our team will call your registered number. This is available to every owner.

Can the support team look at my account?

When you ask for help, a team member may open your account from the admin panel to fix the problem for you. Every such access is recorded. If you have questions, you can contact us.

Payments and refunds

Which payment methods are supported?

Payments are made through UPI and processed by Razorpay.

Is my payment information safe?

Yes. Razorpay is an RBI-authorised payment aggregator, and RentGoX never receives or stores your UPI PIN or bank login details. See our Privacy Policy.

Can I get a refund?

Premium plans and Boosts are digital services activated instantly, so they are non-refundable once active, except for a duplicate payment or a verified activation failure. Please report these quickly, as explained in our Refund & Cancellation Policy. A failed payment that was debited is normally reversed by your bank within 5 to 7 business days.

Money was deducted but my plan or Boost is not active. What should I do?

Wait a few minutes and reopen the app. If it is still not active, email supportrentgox@gmail.com with your registered mobile number and the transaction ID, and we will activate it or refund you.

Do plans renew automatically?

No. Every plan is prepaid for a fixed period and ends on its own, so there is nothing to cancel.

Marriage halls

Can I list or book a marriage hall?

Marriage hall features are being rolled out gradually. Hall owners with an access code can enter it in Settings under Marriage Hall Access. Customers pay the booking advance online, and cancelling within 48 hours of paying it gives a full refund. After 48 hours the advance is non-refundable.

Privacy, security and support

Who can see my phone number?

An owner's contact number is shown on their listings. A seeker's name and details are shared only with the owner they contact. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

How long are my chats kept?

Chat messages are deleted automatically 10 days after they are sent, and a conversation is deleted 10 days after its last message. This is the same for Free and Premium users.

Can I lock the app?

Yes. Go to Settings and open App Lock & Security to set a PIN or use your fingerprint or face. Your biometric data stays on your phone and RentGoX never receives it.

Does the app have a data saver?

Yes. Settings has a Data Saver option that loads photos and videos only when you tap them.

How do I delete my account?

In the app go to Settings and tap Delete Account. This works for both seekers and owners. If you cannot open the app, follow the steps on our Delete Account page. Deletion is permanent.

How do I contact support?

Email supportrentgox@gmail.com or use the form on our Contact page. Support is available Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST, and we aim to reply within 2 business days.

Where can I follow RentGoX?

On Facebook and Instagram.
