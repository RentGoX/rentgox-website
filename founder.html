About RentGoX

The problem

Finding a room, PG, apartment or hall to rent in Tripura has traditionally meant scattered word-of-mouth listings and unverified contacts.

The product

RentGoX brings rooms, PGs, apartments/flats and marriage halls onto one verified, searchable app with in-app chat and direct call/WhatsApp contact.

The starting point

Launched in 2026 from Agartala, Tripura, with plans to expand into vehicles, electronics, furniture and office space rentals.
