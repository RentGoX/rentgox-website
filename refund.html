Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: July 9, 2026

This Refund & Cancellation Policy applies to paid subscription plans and Premium Services purchased by property owners on RentGoX, and forms part of our Terms of Service. It does not cover any rental transaction between an owner and a seeker conducted outside the app — RentGoX is not a party to those arrangements.

1. Premium Subscription Policy

Owners may subscribe to a paid plan or purchase Premium Services to increase the number of properties they can list, gain featured visibility, or access additional features. Plans are billed for the period stated at checkout (for example, 6 months or 1 year) and do not renew automatically — you choose when to purchase again. Features, validity, and pricing of each plan are displayed clearly before purchase.

2. Payment Gateway (PhonePe)

All payments on RentGoX are processed securely through PhonePe Payment Gateway and/or other RBI-approved, PCI-DSS compliant third-party payment processors. RentGoX does not store your full card number, UPI PIN, or net-banking credentials on its own servers.

3. Failed Transactions

If a payment fails or is declined but an amount is debited from your account, the amount is typically auto-reversed by the payment gateway, bank, or UPI provider within 5–7 business days. If it is not reversed automatically within this window, please contact us with your transaction reference for assistance.

4. Duplicate Payments

In case of a duplicate payment for the same plan or service, the extra amount charged is fully refundable. Please report duplicate charges within 7 days along with both transaction reference IDs.

5. Refund Eligibility

SituationOutcome
Duplicate payment or payment deducted but plan not activatedFull refund
Technical error on our end preventing use of the purchased planFull or partial refund, reviewed case by case
Payment failed or declined but amount debited by bank/UPI appAutomatically reversed by the payment gateway/bank, typically within 5–7 business days
Change of mind after a plan is active and functioning normallyNot eligible
Refund request made more than 7 days after purchaseNot eligible, except for unresolved technical issues

6. Non-Refundable Services

The following are generally non-refundable once the service has been successfully activated and delivered:

  • Featured listing placements that have already been displayed, in full or in part.
  • Verified badges or profile enhancements already applied to an account.
  • Any Premium Service consumed or used beyond the eligibility window described above.

7. Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds are credited back to the original payment method used for the transaction, where applicable, within 5–7 business days of approval. Your bank, UPI app, or card issuer may take additional time to reflect the refund in your statement.

8. Cancellation Policy

You may stop using a plan at any time from within the app. Because plans are prepaid for a fixed term, cancelling a plan does not generate a refund for the unused period, except as described in the eligibility table above.

9. Consumer Rights

Nothing in this Policy limits any right or remedy available to you under applicable Indian consumer protection law, including the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.

10. Contact Support

To request a refund or raise a payment issue, email us at supportrentgox@gmail.com or use our Contact Us page, with your registered phone number, the payment/transaction reference ID, and a short description of the issue. We aim to respond within 2 business days.
