Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: November 11, 2026

This Refund & Cancellation Policy applies to payments made on the RentGoX Android app and website for Premium plans, Boosts (Featured listings), Marriage Hall subscriptions, and Marriage Hall booking advances. It forms part of our Terms of Service. It does not cover rent, deposits, or any payment made directly between an owner and a seeker outside the app. RentGoX is not a party to those.

Please Read Carefully
In short: Premium plans, Boosts, and subscriptions are digital services activated immediately and are non-refundable once active, except for duplicate payments and verified activation failures. Marriage hall booking advances are fully refundable if cancelled within 48 hours of payment.

1. What You Can Buy

  • Premium plans (property owners): fixed-term plans, such as 6 months or 1 year, that raise listing limits and add features such as more photos per listing, video upload, unlimited listings on higher plans, and unlimited chat messages. Features, validity, and price are shown before you pay.
  • Boost (Featured listing): a paid option that shows one of your properties in the Featured section for 1, 3, 7, or 15 days.
  • Marriage Hall subscriptions (hall owners): a paid subscription for listing one marriage hall.
  • Marriage Hall booking advance (customers): an advance paid online through the app to book a hall.

Plans do not renew automatically, and no cancellation step is needed to avoid future charges.

2. Payment Gateway

All payments are made through UPI and processed securely by Razorpay, an RBI-authorised payment aggregator. RentGoX never receives or stores your UPI PIN or bank login details.

3. Failed Transactions

If a payment fails or is declined but money is debited from your account, the amount is normally reversed automatically by the payment gateway or your bank within 5 to 7 business days. If it is not reversed in that time, email us with the transaction reference ID and we will help you check with the gateway.

4. Premium Plans and Subscriptions: Refund Eligibility

Premium plans and Marriage Hall subscriptions are activated instantly on payment, so they are non-refundable once successfully activated, except in these cases:

  • Duplicate payment: you were charged more than once for the same purchase. Report it within 24 hours (1 day) with both transaction IDs. After verification, the extra amount is refunded.
  • Payment deducted but service not activated: due to a verified technical issue. Report it within 24 hours (1 day) with the transaction ID. We will either activate your purchase or refund it if activation is not possible.

Refunds are not available for:

  • Change of mind after purchase or activation.
  • Partial use, non-use, or unused validity of an active plan, and plans that have expired.
  • Accounts or listings suspended, hidden, or removed for breaching our Terms.
  • Deleting your account or uninstalling the app.
  • Listings that are rejected for not meeting our listing rules.

If you used a coupon or discount, any refund is limited to the amount you actually paid.

5. Boost (Featured Listing): Refund Eligibility

A Boost starts as soon as your payment is confirmed, so it is non-refundable once active, except in these cases:

  • Duplicate payment: you were charged more than once for the same Boost. Report it within 24 hours (1 day) with both transaction IDs. After verification, the extra amount is refunded.
  • Payment deducted but Boost not started: our system refunds the payment automatically, normally within 5 to 7 business days. If you have not received it, email us within 24 hours (1 day) with the transaction ID.

Refunds are not available when:

  • We stop the Boost because the listing broke our rules or was found to be false or misleading.
  • The property is rented, sold, hidden, or deleted while the Boost is running.
  • Your account is deleted or suspended.
  • You changed your mind, or the Boost did not bring the number of enquiries you expected.

6. Marriage Hall Booking Advance

  • Cancel within 48 hours of paying the advance: you receive a 100% refund of the advance, issued automatically to your original payment method.
  • Cancel after 48 hours: the advance is non-refundable.
  • If an automatic refund cannot be processed, you will be asked to contact support, and we will resolve it.
  • Cancelling a booking, or any refund of a remaining-balance payment made through the app after the 48-hour window, is not automatic. Please email us with your booking details. We will review the request together with the hall owner and the cancellation terms shown at the time of booking.

7. How Refunds Are Processed

Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method used for the purchase. For UPI payments the money goes back to the bank account linked to your UPI ID. It usually reaches your account within 5 to 7 business days after approval, depending on your bank or UPI provider. We cannot send refunds to a different account or method.

8. Cancelling a Plan

You can stop using a Premium plan or subscription at any time. Because plans are prepaid for a fixed term, stopping early does not create a refund for the unused period, except as set out in Section 4. Plans never renew by themselves. A Boost cannot be cancelled once it is active.

9. How to Request a Refund or Report a Problem

Email supportrentgox@gmail.com with:

  • Your registered mobile number.
  • The payment or transaction reference ID, and the date and amount.
  • For a Boost, the name of the property.
  • A short description of the issue and, if possible, a screenshot.

Our support hours are Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST (closed on public holidays). We aim to reply within 2 business days.

10. Your Consumer Rights

Nothing in this policy limits any right or remedy you have under Indian consumer protection law, including the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 and the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may write to us again at supportrentgox@gmail.com and ask for your complaint to be escalated.
