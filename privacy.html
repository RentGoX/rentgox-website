Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 9, 2026

RentGoX ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your information when you use the RentGoX mobile application and related services (the "Service"), and should be read together with our Terms of Service.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Google Sign-In: When you sign in using Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture as authorized by you.
  • Phone Number: Collected during registration for account verification, communication, and security purposes.
  • Property Details: Information and images uploaded by property owners, including address, pricing, and descriptions of listed properties.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and app usage/diagnostic data.
  • Location Permission: Approximate or precise location, collected with your permission, to help renters discover nearby properties.
  • Camera Permission: Used only when you choose to capture and upload photos of a property or your profile picture.
  • Storage Permission: Used to select and upload images or documents from your device's gallery/files.
  • Transaction Information: Payment amount, order reference, and transaction status, collected when you pay for Premium Services.

2. How We Use Information

  • Create, verify, and manage your account.
  • Display and manage your property listings.
  • Help renters find nearby properties using location data.
  • Process payments for Premium Services through our payment gateway partner(s).
  • Send transaction confirmations, receipts, and service-related notifications.
  • Improve app performance, features, and user experience.
  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries.
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents.
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only with:

  • Trusted service providers necessary to operate the app, such as authentication (Google), cloud storage, hosting, and analytics providers.
  • PhonePe and/or other RBI-approved, PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway partners, solely to process your payments securely and confirm transaction status.
  • Government authorities or regulators where required by applicable law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of RentGoX or others.

Payment information: We do not collect or store your full card number, CVV, UPI PIN, or net-banking credentials. These are entered directly into and processed by our payment gateway partner in accordance with their own security standards.

4. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including encryption in transit where applicable, restricted access controls, and secure cloud infrastructure, to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

5. User Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated personal data.
  • Withdraw previously granted permissions (location, camera, storage) via your device settings.
  • Contact us regarding any privacy concerns via our Contact Us page.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to provide our services, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, or comply with legal, tax, and accounting obligations, including transaction record-keeping requirements related to payments.

7. Children's Privacy

RentGoX is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete it.

8. Third-Party Services

RentGoX may use trusted third-party services including Google Sign-In, cloud storage, analytics, mapping/location services, and payment processing (including PhonePe Payment Gateway). These providers may collect and process information according to their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

9. Google Play Compliance

RentGoX complies with the Google Play Developer Program Policies, including the User Data policy and Permissions policy. We only request device permissions (location, camera, storage) that are necessary for core app functionality, and we do not collect sensitive data beyond what is disclosed in this Privacy Policy and our Play Store data safety declaration.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or payment gateway policies. Changes take effect immediately upon publication on this page. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

11. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by and interpreted in accordance with the laws of India, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and applicable data protection and consumer protection regulations.
