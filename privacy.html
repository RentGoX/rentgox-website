Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 11, 2026

RentGoX (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your information when you use the RentGoX mobile application, the RentGoX website, and related services (together, the “Service”). It should be read together with our Terms of Service and Refund & Cancellation Policy.

By using the Service you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use RentGoX (as a room seeker, a property owner, or a marriage hall owner/customer), we may collect:

  • Account information: your name, mobile number, and city/area/state. Property owners sign in with Google, so we also receive your name, email address, and profile picture as authorised by you.
  • Property listing information: photos, video, address, GPS location, rent, deposit, description, amenities, and the contact number you choose to show on a listing.
  • Marriage hall information: hall details, photos, availability, booking details (such as event date and amounts), and, for hall owners, basic business details you submit (full name, mobile number, email, address, business name, business type, and GSTIN if you have one). We do not store your PAN, bank account numbers, or identity documents; any such verification required for settlement is handled directly by our payment partner.
  • Communications: in-app chat messages, visit requests, call requests, property reports you submit, messages you send through our Contact form, and messages you type to the RentGoX AI assistant.
  • Activity and preferences: favourites, saved searches and alerts, and similar choices you make in the app.
  • Payment information: the plan or booking purchased, amount, currency, coupon used, order and payment reference IDs, and payment status. Your UPI payment is completed on your UPI app through our payment gateway, and we never receive or store your UPI PIN or bank details. For a Boost, we also record which property was boosted and for how many days.
  • Device and diagnostic information: device model, operating system version, app version, device identifiers, push-notification token, install referrer, crash reports, and app usage analytics.
  • Location (with your permission): approximate or precise location, used to show nearby properties and to capture a property’s location when an owner lists it.

2. Permissions the App Requests

  • Location: to help you discover nearby properties and to capture a listing’s GPS position. You can turn this off in your device settings.
  • Camera: only when you choose to take a photo for a listing or profile, or to scan a QR code.
  • Notifications: to send visit requests, chat messages, listing updates, and service alerts.
  • Biometrics / screen lock: only if you enable App Lock. Fingerprint or face matching is performed by your device; RentGoX never receives or stores your biometric data.
  • Photos and files: you select images or videos through your device’s picker. We do not request broad access to your storage.
  • Internet and network state: to load listings and keep your account in sync.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Create, verify, and manage your account.
  • Display, review, and manage property and marriage hall listings.
  • Connect seekers with owners through chat, visit requests, and calls.
  • Show nearby properties and nearby places using location data.
  • Process payments for Premium plans, Boosts, subscriptions, and bookings through our payment gateway.
  • Show boosted properties in the Featured section and manage Boost periods.
  • Send transaction confirmations, service-related notifications, and push notifications.
  • Provide customer support, including the RentGoX AI assistant and callback requests.
  • Improve app performance, fix crashes, and understand how features are used.
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, fake listings, abuse, and security incidents.
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

We process your personal data based on your consent and, where applicable, for legitimate uses permitted by Indian law, such as providing the service you asked for, preventing fraud, and meeting legal obligations. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account or by changing the relevant permission on your device, although some features may then stop working.

4. Information Visible to Other Users

RentGoX is a listing platform, so some information is shared with other users by design:

  • Property listings (photos, video, address, price, description, location, and the contact number shown on the listing) can be seen by anyone using the app or website.
  • Properties with an active Boost are shown to all users in the Featured section with a “Featured” label.
  • Your name and profile details may be shown to the other person in a chat, visit request, or booking.
  • Chat messages are visible to the people in that conversation and may be reviewed by RentGoX when needed to investigate a report or abuse.

Please do not post information you do not want to be public in a listing, and do not share sensitive personal information in chats.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as described below:

  • Google / Firebase: sign-in, push notifications, analytics, and crash reporting.
  • Supabase: database and server functions that store account, listing, booking, and payment records.
  • Appwrite: delivery of in-app chat messages.
  • Cloudflare R2: storage of listing photos and videos.
  • Razorpay: processing of payments and refunds, and confirmation of payment status.
  • Google Gemini (RentGoX AI assistant): the messages you type to the assistant are sent to Google’s Gemini service to generate replies. Please do not enter sensitive personal information into the assistant.
  • OpenStreetMap services: map display and nearby-places lookup, which use the coordinates of a property or search area.
  • Web3Forms: delivery of messages you send through the Contact form to our support inbox.
  • Google Play: app distribution and install-referrer information.
  • RentGoX team: authorised team members may access an account for support, security checks, and complaints, and every such access is logged.
  • Authorities: government, law-enforcement, or regulatory bodies where required by law or legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of RentGoX, our users, or others.

These providers process data on our behalf or under their own privacy policies, and may store it on servers located outside India.

6. Payment Security

Payments are made through UPI and processed by Razorpay, an RBI-authorised payment aggregator. We never receive or store your UPI PIN or bank login details. These stay with your UPI app, your bank, and the payment gateway under their own security standards.

7. Data Retention

  • We keep your account and listing information while your account is active.
  • When you delete your account, your personal information, listings, and related data are removed as described in the app’s Delete Account screen.
  • Chat messages are deleted automatically 10 days after they are sent, and a conversation is deleted 10 days after its last message.
  • To prevent misuse of free-listing limits, we retain the mobile number of a deleted owner account together with the number of listings it used.
  • Payment, transaction, and Boost records are retained for as long as required by applicable tax, accounting, and legal obligations.
  • Some information may remain in secure backups for a limited period, or be kept longer where needed to resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, or comply with law.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including encryption in transit, access controls, server-side verification of payments, and secure cloud infrastructure, to protect your information against unauthorised access, alteration, loss, misuse, or disclosure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your account and personal data. You can do this yourself in the app under Settings → Delete Account, or on our website’s Delete Account page.
  • Withdraw permissions (location, camera, notifications) at any time in your device settings.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.
  • Raise a complaint with us (see Section 13).

To make a request, email us from your registered contact details at supportrentgox@gmail.com.

10. Children’s Privacy

RentGoX is intended for people aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. When you sign in, you confirm by ticking the box on the login screen that you are 18 or older. If we learn that a child’s information has been provided to us, we will delete it. If you believe a child has used the Service, please contact us.

11. Third-Party Services and Links

The Service may link to or rely on third-party services such as Google, Razorpay, and map providers. Their handling of your data is governed by their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to read them.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, features, or legal requirements. The updated version will be posted in the app and on our website with a new effective date. Continued use of the Service after a change means you accept the updated policy.
