Delete Your RentGoX Account

You can delete your RentGoX account at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 1: Delete inside the app (fastest)

  • Open the RentGoX app and go to Settings → Delete Account.
  • Read the list of what will be deleted, then confirm with Yes, Delete My Account.
  • Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

  • Send an email to supportrentgox@gmail.com with the subject line Delete My RentGoX Account.
  • Include the registered mobile number or the Google account email used with RentGoX.
  • Our support team will verify your request and process it. Most requests are processed within 7 business days.

What is deleted

  • Account information
  • Saved and favourite properties
  • Search history (if any)
  • Login session

For Owner accounts, this also deletes:

  • All property listings
  • Property images
  • Listing history

This data cannot be recovered under any circumstances.

What we keep after deletion

  • Chat messages are deleted automatically 10 days after they are sent, and a conversation is deleted 10 days after its last message.
  • To prevent misuse of free-listing limits, we retain the mobile number of a deleted owner account together with the number of listings it used.
  • Payment, transaction, and Boost records are retained for as long as required by applicable tax, accounting, and legal obligations.
  • Some information may remain in secure backups for a limited period, or be kept longer where needed to resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, or comply with law.

See our Privacy Policy (Section 7, Data Retention) for details.
